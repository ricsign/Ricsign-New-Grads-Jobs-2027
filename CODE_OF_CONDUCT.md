# Code of Conduct

This is a job board. The stakes for the people using it are real — a wrong
sponsorship flag or a stale deadline costs someone an opportunity.

**Expected:** be accurate, cite sources, assume good faith, and take correction
gracefully. If you are unsure about a fact, say so rather than filling the gap.

**Not accepted:** harassment of any kind; disparaging individual employees of
listed companies; posting private or personal information; using issues to
advertise a product or service; or knowingly submitting inaccurate data.

Report problems by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer through
GitHub. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may ban
accounts for behavior they judge inappropriate.
