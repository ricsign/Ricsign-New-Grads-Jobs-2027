# The Company Bar

Every other new-grad repo optimizes for coverage. This one optimizes for the
opposite. The scarce resource for a strong candidate is not listings — it is
attention, and every irrelevant row spends some.

So the first question this project answers is not "how do we scrape more?" but
"who is even allowed in?"

## The test

A company is eligible if a strong CS candidate with competing offers would take
the interview without needing to be talked into it.

That is subjective. It is written down anyway, because a subjective bar applied
consistently and published for audit beats an objective bar that admits anyone
with an ATS account.

## Tiers

| Tier | Meaning | Examples |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **0** | Frontier AI labs and the firms that define their category. Research-defining work; compensation at the very top of the market. | Anthropic, OpenAI, Jane Street, Citadel, Databricks, Stripe |
| **1** | Established elite. FAANG+, top quant/HFT, category-leading scale-ups. | NVIDIA, Palantir, Jump Trading, Figma, Ramp, Anduril |
| **2** | Strong specialist or earlier-stage companies with a real engineering reputation. Included, ranked below 0 and 1 everywhere they appear. | Grafana, Postman, Nuro, Chime |

Tier drives sort order on every board. Within a tier we sort by recency, never
alphabetically — alphabetical sorting is why competing lists open on Adobe.

## Hard exclusions

These are excluded by policy, not by oversight:

- **Staffing agencies, consultancies, and body shops.** The role is real; the
  employer is not the one you would be working for.
- **Data-annotation and content-labeling vendors.** High-volume posters that
  crowd out everything else. One competing list carries 64 open reqs from a
  single annotation vendor.
- **Companies that post the same requisition across dozens of cities.** We keep
  one row with all locations; we do not keep thirty rows.
- **Unpaid roles, and "internships" that are actually coursework.**
- **Any employer whose posting we cannot resolve to a working apply link.**

## Volume caps

No company may occupy more than **3 rows per board per refresh**. Without a cap,
a single company with a large campus program drowns a board — on one competing
list, TikTok and ByteDance alone account for 8.9% of every live new-grad row.

When a company exceeds its cap, we keep the three most recently posted roles and
link to their full board.

## How to propose a change

Open an issue with the `company` template. Additions need one thing: a short,
concrete argument that the company clears the test above. "They're hiring" is
not an argument.

Removals are welcome too, and are held to the same standard. A company that
stops clearing the bar should leave.

## What we deliberately do not do

**We do not rank companies against each other beyond tiers.** Tier 0 is not a
leaderboard, and the ordering within a tier carries no judgment. Which of these
is the best place to work is a question about you, not about them.
