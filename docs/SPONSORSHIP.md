# Visa Sponsorship — What the Filings Actually Show

If you need sponsorship, this is the first filter you apply to any job list, and
it is the field every competing board gets wrong. On one large competing repo,
the sponsorship field reads `"Other"` on **99.1%** of live rows — the badge
legend is decorative.

Two things here that you will not find elsewhere:

1. Every posting on our boards is parsed for its actual work-authorization
   clause. When the posting is silent we mark it `·` (unknown) rather than
   implying anything.
2. This page cross-checks employer behavior against **public DOL H-1B/LCA
   filings** — what a company actually *did*, not what a job description says.

---

## How to read the badges

| Badge | Meaning | Where it comes from |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 🌏 | Sponsors visas | Posting states sponsorship is offered/available |
| 🛂 | No sponsorship | Posting states it cannot or will not sponsor |
| 🇺🇸 | US citizenship / US person required | Posting states citizenship, LPR, or ITAR "US person" status |
| 🔒 | Security clearance required | Posting requires an active or obtainable clearance |
| `·` | **The posting did not say** | Not a guess. Ask the recruiter. |

`·` is a real answer. Roughly a third of postings genuinely do not address it,
and the honest move is to say so.

Jane Street and HRT sponsor at real volume and filed as recently as December
2025. Whatever you have read on forums, the filings say otherwise.

**Myth: "Palantir won't sponsor."**

[Palantir has 510 H-1B records, including 148 Software Engineer
filings, through September 2025](https://h1bdata.info/index.php?em=palantir+technologies+inc&job=&city=&year=All+Years).
Palantir sponsors at scale for its commercial roles. Its US-Government and
clearance-gated roles are a **separate track** with citizenship requirements —
which is why the badge is per-posting and not per-company.

## Where the restriction is real

| Employer | Signal |
|:--|:--|
| [Anduril](https://h1bdata.info/index.php?em=anduril+industries&job=&city=&year=All+Years) | **5 total H-1B records, zero Software Engineer, nothing filed since Jan 2023.** Consistent with ITAR/citizenship gating across the board. |
| [Optiver US](https://h1bdata.info/index.php?em=optiver+us+llc&job=&city=&year=All+Years) | 17 records, **none since 2021**. |
| SpaceX | Widely reported ITAR citizenship restriction. We did **not** verify this against a primary SpaceX source, so we do not badge it from company policy — only from what each posting says. |

**An important caveat.** Absence of H-1B filings is strong evidence, not proof.
A company may sponsor via O-1, cap-exempt arrangements, or transfers that do not
appear the same way. Treat the table above as a prior, and verify on the
specific posting.

## Practical notes for international students

- **Cap-exempt matters.** University-affiliated labs and some nonprofit research
  institutes are exempt from the H-1B lottery cap. If the lottery is your
  bottleneck, a cap-exempt research position is a different game entirely.
- **STEM OPT gives 36 months.** That is three lottery attempts if your employer
  files each year. Ask explicitly: *"Do you file H-1B petitions every year an
  employee is eligible?"* — some employers only file once.
- **"US Person" ≠ citizen.** Under ITAR, a US Person includes lawful permanent
  residents and certain protected individuals. A posting that says "US Person"
  is not necessarily citizens-only. It is worth asking.
- **Ask the recruiter in writing, early.** Not to be difficult — because a
  recruiter's yes/no in email is worth more than any list, including this one.
