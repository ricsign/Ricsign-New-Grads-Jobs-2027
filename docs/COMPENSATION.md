# Compensation — 2027 New Grad

Every number on this page has a source link. Numbers we could not source are
listed as **unsourced** rather than filled in from SEO blogs, because a wrong
number here changes where someone applies.

**Read this first.** Two different things get called "compensation":

- **Total compensation (TC)** — base + equity/yr + bonus. What people mean by
  "Anthropic pays $375k". Self-reported, so it skews toward people who are
  pleased with their offer.
- **Base salary from DOL filings** — the wage an employer certified to the
  Department of Labor on an H-1B/LCA petition. It is a *legal filing*, not a
  survey, so it is far more reliable — but it is **base only**, excludes equity
  and bonus entirely, and is frequently filed at the bottom of the band.

They are not comparable. Do not average them.

**Three things this table is not telling you.**

1. **Quant totals understate.** HRT, Jane Street, Optiver and DES pay a
   discretionary year-end bonus that is not in these figures. First-year
   realized comp at those firms routinely lands above the number shown.
2. **AI lab equity is illiquid.** Anthropic's and OpenAI's "stock/yr" is
   private-company paper. Its realizable value depends on tender offers, and it
   is not the same asset as Google RSUs.
3. **This is the 99th percentile, not the market.** US entry-level SWE median
   TC was **$155,000** in 2025, up 1.64% YoY
   ([levels.fyi 2025 pay report](https://www.levels.fyi/2025/)). Everything in
   the table above is a tail outcome.

## The PhD premium is real, and it is measurable

Comparing research-track titles against SWE titles **within the same employer's
own filings**, base salary alone:

| Employer | Research track | SWE track | Delta |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|
| Anthropic | $315,000 (MTS) | $250,000 (entry SWE base) | **+$65,000** |
| Jane Street | $300,000 (Researcher/Trader) | $250,000 (SWE) | **+$50,000** |
| Citadel Securities | $185,000 (Quant Researcher) | $165,000 (SWE) | **+$20,000** |

If you are finishing a PhD, the [AI Research board](../boards/AI_RESEARCH.md)
and the research titles on the [Quant board](../boards/QUANT.md) are where that
premium lives. It does not show up on a new-grad SWE requisition.

## Negotiation notes

- **Competing offers move base at quant firms and equity at labs.** Quant firms
  rarely restructure equity because there usually isn't any; labs have more room
  on the equity line than on base.
- **Ask what the equity is actually worth.** For a private lab: most recent
  409A, most recent preferred price, whether tender offers have happened, and
  the post-termination exercise window. A four-year grant with a 90-day exercise
  window is worth materially less than one with a 10-year window.
- **Sign-on is the softest line item** and the least likely to set an internal
  precedent, which is why it moves first.
- **Watch the bonus structure at quant firms.** A $300k base with a
  discretionary bonus is a different risk profile than a guaranteed first-year
  number. Ask what the bonus range was for last year's analyst class.

*Nothing here is financial advice. These are public data points and common
practice, not a recommendation about any specific offer.*
